# What's new

*Changes to ProspectIQ, newest first. One entry per shipped feature.*

## 16 October 2026

- **Docs split by audience**: clients see only the client guide, with no link out of Launchpad. The overview, admin guide and changelog open with the admin sign-in or the team docs link from Settings.
- **Screenshots** in the product overview, client guide and admin guide, and a ProspectIQ icon in the browser tab.
- **Email waterfall page** in admin: hit rate, verified share, cost per email and speed from every lookup, plus a tester that runs the waterfall on a pasted list or a Scout search without pushing anything.
- **Documentation** lives in the app: product overview, client guide, admin guide and this page, with a Guide link in the client header and a Docs link in the admin sidebar.

## 15 October 2026

- **Scout** is the new name of the first tab. It was called Ask; the rename keeps it apart from Launchpad's own Ask AI.
- **Finders at a time**: at most two finders run per account at once, adjustable per client in admin.
- **Email waterfall**: a second email source for people the research database has no email for, with a time budget per push. Off by default, switched on per client.
- **Admin feed**: the Overview activity feed is paged and filterable by kind and client.
- **Costs page**: monthly spend by account, feature, model and day.
- **API keys in Settings**: the research database, AI and waterfall keys are managed from the dashboard and shown masked.
- **Top up**: one click restores a client to the full monthly allowance.

## 14 October 2026

- **Daily cap on automatic pushes**: finders add up to 50 leads a day per account, counted in the client's timezone. The limit and its progress are shown on the finders list.
- **Client UI pass**: one clear hierarchy per screen, highlighted pills, tidier result cards.

## 13 October 2026

- **Activity log** on a client tab and across clients in admin.
- **Admin app** with a sidebar, Overview and dark mode.
- **Monthly credits** that reset on the 1st in the client's timezone, bonus credits, a support email shown when credits run out.
- **One menu link for every sub-account**, matched by location id.
- **Already in Launchpad** detection on results, skipped by finders.
- **First release** of the Node and React rebuild: multi-client, with an admin panel, served at js.launchpadinnovations.ai/prospectiq, deployed automatically on every push to main.
